NetPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetPilot handles information when you use the NetPilot mobile application.

1. About NetPilot

NetPilot is a network diagnostic and internet speed testing application. It provides information such as download speed, upload speed, latency, jitter, and information about the device's current network connection.

2. Information You Do Not Need to Provide

NetPilot does not require you to create an account.

NetPilot does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, camera access, microphone access, financial information, or payment information.

3. Network and Wi-Fi Information

NetPilot may access information about the network to which your device is currently connected, including the Wi-Fi network name (SSID), where permitted by Android.

Android may require location permission to allow an application to access certain Wi-Fi information such as the SSID.

NetPilot uses this permission for network diagnostic purposes. NetPilot does not use this permission to track your physical movements.

4. Internet Speed Test Information

NetPilot performs network measurements including:

  • Download speed
  • Upload speed
  • Latency
  • Jitter

Speed test history stored by NetPilot is stored locally on your device.

5. Firebase Services

NetPilot uses Google Firebase services for application reliability, crash diagnostics, and analytics.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information when NetPilot crashes or encounters a serious application error.

This information may include:

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Application state
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Application version information
  • Installation identifiers

Firebase Analytics

NetPilot includes Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics may collect technical and application-usage information such as:

  • Device information
  • Application instance identifiers
  • Session information
  • General application activity

NetPilot does not currently assign Firebase user IDs, create user profiles, or associate Firebase analytics information with a NetPilot user account.

6. Data Sharing

NetPilot does not sell your personal information.

Certain technical, analytics, or diagnostic information may be processed by Google Firebase as a service provider for application analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and application reliability.

7. Data Retention

Speed test history stored locally by NetPilot may remain on your device until the data is removed, the application's data is cleared, or NetPilot is uninstalled.

Information processed by Firebase is retained according to the applicable Firebase service configuration and Google's data retention practices.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to information handled by NetPilot. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

NetPilot is a general-purpose network utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Advertising

The current version of NetPilot does not contain an advertising SDK.

If advertising or additional third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable application-store disclosures will be updated accordingly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NetPilot features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data-handling practices change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will remain available at this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or NetPilot's privacy practices, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the NetPilot Google Play Store listing.
