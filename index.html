NetPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetPilot handles information when you use the NetPilot mobile application.

1. About NetPilot

NetPilot is a network diagnostic and internet speed testing application. It provides information such as download speed, upload speed, latency, jitter, and information about the device's current network connection.

2. Information You Do Not Need to Provide

NetPilot does not require you to create an account.

NetPilot does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photographs, camera access, microphone access, financial information, or payment information.

If you choose to make an optional in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. NetPilot never receives or stores your card or payment details (see section 6).

3. Network and Wi-Fi Information

NetPilot may access information about the network to which your device is currently connected, where permitted by Android. This can include the connection type (Wi-Fi, mobile or other), the Wi-Fi network name (SSID), the identifier of the Wi-Fi access point (BSSID), the frequency band and channel, signal strength, link speed, and the name of your mobile carrier.

NetPilot saves these network details together with each speed test so that it can group your results by network and by access point (for example, a router and a repeater). These details are stored only on your device (see section 4).

Android requires location permission before an application can read certain Wi-Fi information such as the SSID and BSSID. NetPilot asks for this permission only while you are using the app, uses it only to read network details for diagnostics, and does not record, store or send your physical location or track your movements. If you do not grant the permission, NetPilot still works and saves results without network names.

Certain third-party services used by NetPilot may also process approximate location information derived from network or device information as described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy.

4. Speed Test History, Notes, Profiles and Reports

NetPilot performs network measurements including:

  • Download speed
  • Upload speed
  • Latency
  • Jitter

Your speed test history, the network details saved with it, notes you write, profiles you create (for example a home, site or customer name, type, address and notes you enter), names you give to access points, reports (PDF files) you generate, and your app settings are stored locally on your device. NetPilot does not upload this information to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

If you enter information about other people, such as a customer's name or address, in a profile or report, you are responsible for having the right to store and share it.

A report is only shared when you choose to share or save it, using Android's share or save options. Where it goes is decided by you and by the app or service you choose.

Device backup. NetPilot supports Android's automatic backup. If backup is turned on for your device and Google account, Android may include NetPilot's history, profiles, reports and settings in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account under Google's own terms. This allows the data to be restored when you reinstall NetPilot or set up a new device with the same account. The developer cannot access these backups. You can turn device backup off in your device settings.

5. Google and Firebase Services

NetPilot uses certain Google services for application reliability, analytics, and advertising.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information when NetPilot crashes or encounters a serious application error.

This information may include:

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Application state
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Application version information
  • Installation identifiers

Firebase Analytics

NetPilot includes Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics may collect technical and application-usage information such as:

  • Device information
  • Application instance identifiers
  • Session information
  • General application activity

NetPilot does not currently assign Firebase user IDs, create user profiles, or associate Firebase Analytics information with a NetPilot user account.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

NetPilot uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process technical information associated with the device, application, network connection, and advertising activity.

Depending on the device, region, applicable permissions, consent requirements, and Google configuration, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Device and operating system information
  • Application information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be processed for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

6. In-App Purchases

NetPilot may offer optional one-time purchases, such as "Remove Ads Forever", through Google Play Billing.

Payments, payment methods, refunds and receipts are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. NetPilot does not receive or store your payment card or other payment details.

To recognise and restore your purchases, NetPilot receives purchase information from Google Play, such as the product purchased, the purchase status, a purchase token, and a signature that proves the purchase is genuine. NetPilot verifies this information and stores a record of your purchases locally on your device. This record is not sent to the developer or to any other server.

You can restore purchases on a new or reset device with the "Restore purchases" option in the app's Settings, which asks Google Play for your existing purchases.

7. Data Sharing

NetPilot does not sell your personal information.

Certain technical, analytics, diagnostic, device, advertising, or approximate-location information may be processed by Google through services used by NetPilot, including Firebase and Google Mobile Ads.

These services may process information for purposes such as application analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, application reliability, advertising, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

8. Data Retention

Speed test history, notes, profiles, reports and the local record of your purchases remain on your device until you delete them, the application's data is cleared, or NetPilot is uninstalled. Tests you move to the Recycle Bin are deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier if you delete them. A copy may also remain in your Android device backup, if backup is turned on, until that backup is replaced or deleted. Your purchases themselves remain linked to your Google account by Google Play and can be restored.

Information processed by Google services, including Firebase and Google Mobile Ads, may be retained according to the applicable service configuration, Google's data retention practices, and applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to information handled by NetPilot. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

NetPilot is a general-purpose network utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Advertising

NetPilot displays advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including banner ads and interstitial ads.

Banner advertisements may appear within the NetPilot interface. An interstitial advertisement may appear when you choose to start another test after a completed test. Your completed results are not covered by an advertisement.

NetPilot does not intentionally interrupt an active speed test with an interstitial advertisement.

If you purchase "Remove Ads Forever", NetPilot stops showing banner and interstitial advertisements on devices where the purchase is recognised.

Google Mobile Ads may process certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Advertising behavior, including whether advertisements are personalized or non-personalized, may depend on the user's region, device settings, consent choices, applicable law, and Google's advertising configuration.

NetPilot does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Information processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies, advertising technologies, and legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NetPilot features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data-handling practices change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will remain available at this page.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or NetPilot's privacy practices, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the NetPilot Google Play Store listing.
